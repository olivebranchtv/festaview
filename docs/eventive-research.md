# Eventive research (2026-11-19)

Source: https://eventive.org/film-festivals and related Eventive pages (verify current terms before quoting).

## What Eventive offers festivals
- Ticketing and box office: passes, single tickets, rush line, pay-what-you-can; desktop/mobile/iPad box office,
  scanner and terminal hardware, printed passes with QR codes; online, at the door, on site.
- Pass rules and benefits: configurable, automatic passholder/member/sponsor benefits without discount codes.
- Film guide and festival website: searchable, genre-segmented, one-click reservations; no-code page editor; API/CSS.
- Virtual and hybrid: Eventive Virtual (VOD, live streaming, chat, DRM), Eventive TV app, same tools in person or online.
- Audience data and marketing: dashboards, Mailchimp / Salesforce / Zapier / Google Analytics integrations,
  one-click messages to sponsors, passholders, members and donors.
- Sponsors, donors, members: showcase, donations at checkout, automatic benefits.
- Pricing as stated: 5% + $0.99 per paid item (card fees included); free tickets, cash, donations carry no fee;
  fees can be passed to customers; multiple currencies. Virtual has extra fees (third-party sources mention DRM unlock
  fees and hourly live-stream costs; a $299 single-film release option exists for filmmakers).
- Not described on the page: submissions, jury/balloting detail, payout timing, filmmaker tools.

## How Festaview compares
Festaview already covers: branded festival page, film guide with search and genres, schedule, passes link,
VOD, live and automatic channels, TV app (Roku), analytics, theater screening tools, audience voting.
Festaview's difference: ad-supported free viewing with an 80% festival share, scheduled TV channels, and a shared
network audience. Eventive is ticketing-first.

## Gaps worth closing (ideas, in rough priority)
1. Real checkout flow tested end to end; pay-what-you-can and donation at checkout.
2. Pass rules (e.g. pass tiers, limits per film, member/sponsor benefits).
3. QR tickets and a scanner/box office for in-person screenings (large).
4. Audience email: Mailchimp / Zapier integrations; messages to passholders.
5. Sponsor and donor showcase pages.
6. DRM or signed expiring links for ticketed streams.
7. Currency options beyond USD.
8. Fee model: decide Festaview's cut (Eventive benchmark: 5% + $0.99 all-in). Stripe's own fees are about 2.9% + 30 cents.
