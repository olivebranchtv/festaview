Assumptions: 4 ad slots per viewer-hour, 70% fill, $12 effective CPM => ~$0.034 gross per viewer-hour.
Festival share 80% => ~$0.027 per viewer-hour.

| Festival size | Monthly viewers | Hours/viewer | Total hours | Gross | Festival (80%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tiny | 500 | 2 | 1,000 | ~$34 | ~$27 |
| Small | 2,000 | 2.5 | 5,000 | ~$168 | ~$134 |
| Mid | 10,000 | 3 | 30,000 | ~$1,008 | ~$806 |
| Large | 100,000 | 4 | 400,000 | ~$13,440 | ~$10,752 |

Network example: 1M viewer-hours/month => ~$33,600 gross; festivals ~$26,900; Festaview ~$6,700.

Takeaways: ad share alone is modest for small festivals; add SaaS fee ($99-$499/mo tiers), direct sponsors,
and ticketed premieres. Sensitivity: each $1 of CPM moves results ~8%.
